# Layered locomotion · Alpha 0.31.0 (nicht aktiv in 0.31.1)

**Status:** Die hier beschriebenen getrennten Kleidungs- und Bein-Ebenen werden in der aktuellen Version nicht mehr zur Charakterdarstellung verwendet. Die Live-Figuren nutzen wieder vollständige Walk-/Idle-Frames, weil der Mix aus statischen Gewändern und einzeln projizierten Beinen sichtbar unpassende Proportionen verursachte. Die Artefakte bleiben für eine spätere Überarbeitung im Repository, sind aber keine aktiv eingesetzten Spielgrafiken.

`garments.png` contains 24 complete, leg-free body/garment layers: six classes, four directions. Their natural alpha silhouettes retain the centre and hem of robes and the complete capes. No rectangular hip mask is used. `src/gait-body-frames.js` records the UVs and stable roots. `tools/pack-gait-bodies.cjs` extracts and packs this layer sheet, removing disconnected source-cell fragments. The shared 448×672 palette PNG is approximately 67 KiB.

`steel.png`, `leather.png`, `brass.png` each contain three rows (front/down, right, back/up) of eight authored single-leg poses. Left profiles mirror the limb art, but the world-space foot target is converted into mirrored local coordinates before drawing. Both legs run half a cycle apart. The complete rendered actor, including robes and weapons, must show the alternating visible feet.

Stance feet move backwards relative to the actor at exactly the simulated travel speed; swing feet clear the floor and return on a smooth curve. The stride is 60 pixels in vertical views and 72 sideways, interpolated for diagonals. It advances from actual post-collision displacement. Boot width and height stay stable: a continuous source scanline separates the upper limb from its boot, so projected limb length can change without inflating the boot. Starting/stopping blends to a two-foot stance over roughly 0.08 seconds. Pause, hitstop and blocked motion do not advance the distance clock.

Idle and walking share the same body layers. A one-pixel body weight shift and gentle connected-row cloth deformation are cached by pose; they never remove garment pixels. Only the current class's cached cloth poses are retained. There are no per-frame pixel reads. Attack, special, dash, hurt and death continue to use their complete existing action sprites; this update does not replace those animations with a static body or alter their hit/release events.

## Art provenance

The existing limb kits were authored using the built-in Imagegen tool as eight distinct knee/ankle/boot poses per view. The new garment sheet was also made with the built-in Imagegen tool, editing a 4-column by 6-row reference assembled from the existing class sprites. Final editing prompt: preserve each class's identity, pixel style, scale, direction, head, torso, arms, weapons and the entire natural gold-trimmed robe/cape hem; remove all thighs, knees, shins and boots to transparent alpha; keep the front/right/back/left column order and knight/ranger/wizard/gunner/summoner/alchemist row order; no text, grid or background. Source: `exec-1984647c-1f1c-4610-9070-e576e8459ca1.png`. Production asset: `assets/locomotion/garments.png`.

## Review and regression coverage

The browser check renders every class/facing across eight phases, compares the full garment layer with the final composite, checks bounded head drift and verifies that the visible leading foot swaps sides. A second render without limbs excludes weapon pixels from the foot measurement (the downward rifle otherwise resembles a third foot). Unit checks cover stationary world-space support feet in eight travel directions and the actual mirrored draw transform. `tools/preview-gait.cjs` produces a 60-fps, actual-speed moving-floor review of all six classes, including starting and stopping. It requires a local HTTP server and Playwright; its generated captures are QA output, not runtime dependencies.
