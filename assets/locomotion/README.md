# Locomotion limb atlases · Alpha 0.29.0

`steel.png`, `leather.png`, `brass.png`: 512×192 transparent, palette PNGs. Three rows (front/down, right profile, back/up), eight separately drawn single-leg poses per row. Left profiles mirror the right limb kit; they still use the complete eight-pose cycle. Knight uses steel, glaskanonier dark brass/leather armor, the other four classes brown leather boots. Only the selected class’s small kit loads.

The renderer draws two independently timed legs four phases apart. The stance/swing retargeting fixes the hip attachment, sole height and foot trajectory, while retaining the drawn knee/ankle flexion. A full stride advances every 72 travelled world pixels, using actual post-collision displacement. The upper body retains its authored class/action frames. Its walking head/hip placement is normalized separately, and old lower-body pixels are masked. Outer cloak tails and the knight’s outward sword remain visible. The knight’s narrow sword-blade UV polygons are restored after the lower-body mask; two short foreshortened blade crops use reviewed polygons. The gun/bow layer is drawn after the moving legs when aiming downwards.

`tools/pack-locomotion.cjs` packs the source crops and records each hip and foot axis plus stable upper-body head roots in class manifests. It requires Sharp offline; the game uses only ordinary Canvas 2D drawImage, clipping and affine transforms. No per-frame pixel reads or image processing occur in the game.

Artwork was created with the built-in Imagegen tool, using a transparent single-leg anatomy guide. Final prompt set: exactly eight columns by three rows of one detached leg from hip through thigh, bent knee, shin and boot; front/right/back views; eight chronological stance/toe-off/bent passing/extension poses; no second leg, pelvis, torso, head, weapons, text, grid, floor, or background; detailed dark fantasy pixel art with alpha. Material variants: steel plate with gold trim; dark brown leather trousers/strapped boots; charcoal leather with brass-trimmed knee/greave. The earlier full-body candidate sheets were rejected because their front/back legs did not alternate reliably; they are not shipped.

Validation probes the decoded limb raster at opposite phases to verify left/right support changes. It also renders all 24 class/facing combinations across eight steps and while attacking in Chromium and WebKit, checking distinct lower-body pixels, bounded head drift and retained sword pixels in moving attacks. Movement tests cover distance clocks, blocked movement, stun, pause, idle, dash and independent action clocks.
